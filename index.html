Research

Global Patent Network & Technology Innovation Research Group

Team Member

Advisors: Prof. Tang Taijie and Prof. Xu Yonghao

September 2026 – Present

I extract IPC codes, assignees, and citation links from global patent records in Python. I constructed forward citation measures for cross-country comparisons, accounting for changes in citation coverage over time.

I am also testing a Python pipeline that uses TF-IDF and cosine similarity to develop forward and backward measures of patent text similarity.

Determinants of Firms’ Green Innovation Choices

Team Member

Advisor: Prof. Tang Taijie

September 2025 – Present

I built Python scripts to collect annual reports from NEEQ-listed firms, ESG reports from Chinese A-share firms, and papers published in Review of Accounting Studies from 2000 to 2013.

I also replicated and updated tables from two Journal of Corporate Finance studies on Chinese corporate governance, capital markets, and corporate financial policies. I presented papers on innovation, governance, and empirical methods in research group meetings.

Smart Logistics and Environmental Performance

Team Lead · University-funded study

Advisor: Prof. Xiangnian Kong

October 2024 – November 2025

I led a study using 2010–2023 CSMAR manufacturing data and a difference-in-differences design to estimate the effect of China’s 2016 smart-logistics pilot on firms’ environmental ESG performance.

We examined cost reduction, earnings-risk control, and green innovation as possible channels, assessed differences by firm size and region, and ran parallel-trends and placebo tests. The team received an award at Nanjing University’s Yinxing Cup.

China–Malaysia Field Research on Belt and Road Investment

Team Member

Advisor: Prof. Jianghuai Zheng

August 2025

Co-authored a field report on local hiring and technology transfer in Malaysia. We documented how Alliance Steel recruited some technical staff from China and how CETSC trained new hires in China before pairing them with experienced colleagues in Malaysia.

The report won third prize in Nanjing University’s second “Mantianxing” Field Research Report Competition.
